## Suzy Says! walkthrough: the whole game, step by step

Image: MOONBIT, from the game's itch.io page

One run of Suzy Says! takes about twenty minutes. The story beats below come in the same order in every recorded playthrough we checked; the commands between them are random. Spoilers from here on.

Controls: WASD to move, Space to jump, C to crouch.

### 1. Coming home

The game opens in October 2003. You have spent the evening at a friend’s house and get home at 9:00 PM.
- First objective: place your school bag in your room.
- Go to the kitchen and read the note on the counter. Mom is at work until about 11, there is pizza, and your uncle has sent over something he found at work.
- Pick up the Suzy Says box next to the note.
- In your bedroom, turn on the TV and set up the game: the mat goes on the floor and the DVD goes in the player.

### 2. The practice round

Suzy asks your name, then asks you onto the mat and explains the one rule:

The practice is always the same four lines:
- Suzy says, jump on red.
- Suzy says, jump on green.
- Jump on blue. She didn’t say “Suzy says”, so stay where you are.
- Suzy says, jump on yellow.

Then: Okay, let’s play for real.

### 3. The errands

From here the game is rounds of random commands on the mat, each round ending with an errand somewhere in the house. The errands always come in this order. The full list of commands is on the [commands](https://suzysaysguide.com/commands) page.
| Suzy says | Where | What happens |
| Touch the sunflower painting in the living room | Living room | She then calls you back: “Suzy says, come back to the mat.” |
| Touch the rubber duck in the bathroom | Bathroom | When you return she has changed: “It’s pretty dark out there, huh? Do you feel it calling to you? I know I do.” |
| Look at the drawing on the fridge | Kitchen | Your childhood drawing now has a tall dark figure in it. “A special friend wants to play, too.” |
| Close the front door | Hallway | “We have a visitor.” The door is standing open. Close it; she says, “Come back to me.” |

Go, do the thing, and come straight back. Suzy keeps giving commands while you are away from the mat.

### 4. The phone call

The phone rings. Answer it: it is Mom checking on you, and the line breaks up. The call drops and the lights go out. Turn the light switches back on; the tall figure shows itself for a moment and is gone.

Back at the TV:

### 5. “Suzy says, hide”

The next round ends with one word: hide. The lights fail and the tall figure walks the house looking for you.
- Leave the mat, get out of sight and crouch with C.
- In the recorded runs players crouched under a table or behind furniture and did not move. It lasts a little over a minute.
- One player who walked out into the open was grabbed and had to try again; being caught does not lead to a separate ending.

When it is over, go back to the mat:

### 6. The last round

The final round is the longest and mixes mat commands with trips around the house: touch the rubber duck, touch the fridge drawing, look at that sunflower painting, jump on the bed, jump on the couch. Many of them come without “Suzy says”.

For the ones that are real you have to run to the room and back before the next command, so listen for the two words first and move second.

### 7. Four candles
- Find four candles around the house. One is in the bathroom, beside the toilet; we have not confirmed where the other three are.
- Put one on each corner of the mat.
- Suzy says: Look at these symbols.

That starts the ritual ending. If you own the full version, this is also the moment to walk to the living-room radio instead: see the [secret ending](https://suzysaysguide.com/secret-ending).

Checked against recorded playthroughs by [KeeTee](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qr44HHbpjYs), [CatPlay](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7CLLI0ijVGQ), [FoxPlay](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=atUHEpaPZw0), [Nocom](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mWNTwmJWc4I), [Pecan](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-G2Img7S8ug) and [FusionZGamer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a-9y4JkYFDQ). ## Suzy Says! commands: every instruction and what to do

Image: MOONBIT, from the game's itch.io page

Suzy gives one instruction at a time. You act only when the line starts with “Suzy says”. This is every command heard across the recorded playthroughs we checked.

### What is the rule in Suzy Says!?

It is Simon Says. In Suzy’s words:
- “Suzy says, jump on red”: jump on red.
- “Jump on red”: do nothing. If you move she answers, “Oops, I didn’t say Suzy says. You have to listen, silly.”

A wrong move costs a strike, shown as an X. Endless mode shows three X marks. We have not confirmed what the third strike does in the story.

### The full list of commands
| Command | What to do |
| Jump on red / green / blue / yellow | Jump (Space) onto that circle of the mat. |
| Look at me | Turn to face Suzy on the TV. |
| Look at the floor | Look straight down. |
| Look at the ceiling | Look straight up. |
| Look at the window | Turn to the bedroom window. |
| Look behind you | Turn around, away from the TV. |
| Crouch down | Press C. |
| Jump on the bed | The bed in your room. |
| Jump on the couch | The red couch in the living room. |
| Touch the sunflower painting / Look at that sunflower painting | Living room. |
| Touch the rubber duck | Bathroom. |
| Look at the drawing on the fridge / Touch the fridge drawing | Kitchen. |
| Come back to the mat / Come back to me | Return to the mat in your room. |
| Close the front door | Hallway. Story command, once. |
| Hide | Leave the mat, get out of sight and crouch. Story command, once. |
| Bring me four candles | Find four candles and put one on each corner of the mat. Story command, once. |
| Look at these symbols | The last command of the ritual ending. |

### Controls
| W A S D | Move |
| Space | Jump |
| C | Crouch |

### Do you have to stay on the mat?

Yes, according to [Pecan’s recorded run](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-G2Img7S8ug): an icon in the bottom-right corner shows whether you are on the mat, and commands were marked wrong while he stood off it, even ones that only asked him to look somewhere.

So after an errand, get back onto the mat before the next line, and stay on it while you ignore a trap.

### Why do players get strikes?
- Moving on the first words. Wait until you have heard whether the line began with “Suzy says”.
- Following a house command that was a trap. “Touch the rubber duck” without the two words means stay put.
- Being too slow on a real house command. The bathroom and kitchen are a run away, and the next command does not wait.
- Looking away after “Look at me” because something moved. Jumpscares are timed at random.

## Suzy Says! all endings and how to get each one

Image: MOONBIT, from the game's itch.io page

Three endings have been recorded in Suzy Says!. Videos call them good, bad, true and secret in different combinations, so this page names them by what you do. Full spoilers.

### How many endings are there?
| Ending | How to get it | Version |
| The ritual | Play to the end and place the four candles | Free and full |
| Snap the DVD | Take the disc out of the player and snap it | Seen in the full version; not confirmed in the free one |
| Secret ending | Hear the radio messages, enter the colour code on the mat, then snap the disc | Full only |

### Ending 1: the ritual

This is where a normal run ends. Follow the [walkthrough](https://suzysaysguide.com/walkthrough) to the scavenger hunt, put a candle on each corner of the mat, and Suzy gives her last command:

You wake in a black, empty space with a TV. Back in the house the front door opens and Mom calls out:

The game shows your score and thanks you for playing. Looking away from the symbols does not change the result.

### Ending 2: snap the DVD

The disc can be taken back out of the player. When you are holding it the objective becomes “Snap the DVD”, and snapping it ends the game with a score screen.
- Players have done this in the middle of a round and at the very start, before Suzy has finished her introduction.
- After you have finished the game once, a new run can open with “Snap the DVD” as its first objective instead of the school bag.
- Every recording we found of this ending uses the full version. We have not confirmed whether the free file lets you remove the disc.

### Ending 3: the secret ending

Full version only. A man’s voice on the living-room radio gives you a six-colour code. Jump it out on the mat and Suzy loses control of the game:

Then you take the disc out and snap it. The steps and the code are on the [secret ending](https://suzysaysguide.com/secret-ending) page.

### What is the “bad ending”?

The game does not label its endings on screen in any recording we checked, so the word means different things in different videos. Some use it for the ritual; others use it for a run that is cut short during the command rounds.

We have not confirmed what triggers that cut-short run. Being caught during hide and seek is not it: in the one recording where a player was caught on purpose, the game did not treat it as an ending.

## Suzy Says! secret ending: the radio and the colour code

Image: MOONBIT, from the game's itch.io page

The secret ending is in the full version ($1.50 or more on itch.io). The code is red, yellow, red, green, blue, yellow. You get it from the radio in the living room and enter it by jumping on the mat. Full spoilers.
- 1. red
- 2. yellow
- 3. red
- 4. green
- 5. blue
- 6. yellow

### What is the secret code in Suzy Says!?

Red, yellow, red, green, blue, yellow. Six jumps on the mat, in that order.

The code is read out by a man’s voice on the radio. It is the last of five messages.

### Where is the radio, and when does it talk?

The radio is in the living room. Each time the story sends you out of your bedroom, a new message is waiting on it. Interact with the radio to hear it.
| When | What the man says |
| At the start, while Suzy is introducing herself | “The creature. Don’t invite it in.” |
| After “close the front door” | “It’s already in your house. It’s coming for you, too.” |
| After the phone call | “I’m not insane. I had to do it.” |
| After hide and seek | “I still remember their faces… and the smell.” |
| After “bring me four candles” | “Red, yellow, red, green, blue, yellow.” |

The timings are from [Pecan’s recorded run](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-G2Img7S8ug), where each message played on the next visit after the one before it. In his earlier attempt he only heard the first message and never got the code, so collect them in order.

### How do I get the secret ending, step by step?
- Start the full version and play as normal. Keep your strikes down: you still have to survive every round.
- Visit the living-room radio at each of the five moments in the table above.
- When Suzy asks for four candles, skip the candles. Go to the radio and hear the code.
- Go back to the mat and jump on red, yellow, red, green, blue, yellow.
- Suzy shouts at you to stop, and the TV shows an error.
- Take the DVD out of the player and snap it.

### Can I just enter the code without the radio?

Not confirmed. Every recording of the secret ending we found plays the radio messages first. If you try the code cold and it works, tell us and we will update this page.

### What does the secret ending mean?

The game does not say who the man is. What it gives you: someone had this disc before you, saw the same thing, did something he is not proud of, and left a way to stop Suzy hidden on the disc itself.

Players point at the uncle, because Mom’s note says he found the game at work. That is a theory. More on the [story](https://suzysaysguide.com/story) page.

Checked against recorded playthroughs by [KeeTee](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qr44HHbpjYs), [CatPlay](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7CLLI0ijVGQ), [FoxPlay](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=atUHEpaPZw0), [Nocom](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mWNTwmJWc4I), [Pecan](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-G2Img7S8ug) and [FusionZGamer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a-9y4JkYFDQ). ## Suzy Says! story explained: Suzy, the special friend and the man on the radio

Image: MOONBIT, from the game's itch.io page

Suzy Says! tells very little outright. This page separates what the game shows from what players think it means. Full spoilers, including the secret ending.

### What happens in Suzy Says!?
- October 2003. A child gets home at 9 PM. Mom is working until 11 and has left a note.
- The note says an uncle sent over something he found at work: an interactive DVD game with a floor mat.
- The host, Suzy, plays Simon Says with you, then starts naming things in your house: the sunflower painting, the rubber duck, the drawing on the fridge.
- The drawing has gained a tall dark figure. Suzy calls it “a special friend”.
- The front door opens by itself. Mom phones, the line dies, the lights go out, and the figure appears in the house.
- Suzy says “we” about the hunt: “We couldn’t find you anywhere.”
- Four candles on the corners of the mat finish a ritual. You end up in a dark void; Mom comes home to an empty house.

### Who is Suzy?

On screen: a red-haired cartoon girl in a purple dress, the host of a children’s DVD game. She knows the layout of your house, knows where you are standing, and can switch the lights off.

After the rubber duck her tone changes:

Theory: Suzy was a real child taken by the creature, and now fronts the game to bring it more children. The line above and the images that flash on the TV are the evidence people cite. The game does not confirm it.

### What is the “special friend”?

A tall, black, faceless figure. It first appears in your own drawing on the fridge, coming toward the house; then at the front door; then inside, during the blackout and the hide and seek.

The radio voice calls it “the creature” and says it has to be invited in. The game gives it no other name.

### What are the images on the TV?

For a few moments the TV stops showing Suzy and flashes other pictures. Players describe a forest, a child, and what looks like the aftermath of a killing. The game never returns to them. Theory: they show what happened to Suzy.

### Who is the man on the radio?

In the full version, a radio in the living room plays five short messages in a man’s voice:

Then he reads the [colour code](https://suzysaysguide.com/secret-ending) that breaks Suzy’s game.

Theory: he is the uncle. The only thing the game says about the uncle is that he found the disc at work and passed it on. Nothing in the game names the voice.

### Has the developer explained it?

Not that we have found. The two [devlog posts](https://moonbit.itch.io/suzy-says/devlog) cover a subtitle fix and new languages, and end with “More updates to come!”. Players in the itch.io comments are asking for a sequel that explains Suzy and the creature.

## Can you play Suzy Says! online, on Mac or on mobile?

No. Suzy Says! is a Windows download from itch.io. As of 14 November 2026 the developer lists no browser, Mac, Linux, Android, iOS or console version.

### Is there a browser version?

No. The [official page](https://moonbit.itch.io/suzy-says) offers two zip files for Windows and has no “Run game” button, which is what itch.io shows for games that play in the browser.

Sites that promise “Suzy Says unblocked” or “play online” are not the developer’s. What they run, if anything, is not this game.

### Is there a Suzy Says! APK or iPhone app?

No. MOONBIT has not released the game for Android or iOS and does not share an APK. An app with a similar name in a store is not this game.

### Can I play it on a Mac or Chromebook?

Not officially: only a Windows build exists. We have not tested it under Wine, CrossOver or a virtual machine, and the developer does not mention them.

### Is Suzy Says! on Steam?

A Steam store search for “Suzy Says” on 14 November 2026 returned no game. An earlier MOONBIT game, Mr. Hopp’s Manor Escape, is on Steam, and players have asked for this one in the itch.io comments; we found no reply.

### No Windows PC?

You can watch it instead. The game is about twenty minutes long, and no-commentary runs such as [KeeTee’s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qr44HHbpjYs) show all of it. The developer’s own [trailer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jCvkG7amBQ4) is one minute.

### Is Suzy Says! like Amanda the Adventurer?

That is the comparison players and video titles make most: a cheerful children’s show on an old TV that talks to you and turns on you.

The difference is what you do. Amanda the Adventurer is played by answering the tape and solving puzzles around an attic. Suzy Says! is a reaction game: you move your body through a house on command, against the clock.

### Is Suzy Says! based on a real game?

There was no real “Suzy Says” DVD game from 2003 that we could find. The idea is familiar, though: interactive DVD games and plug-in dance mats were common toys in the early 2000s, and the game’s box and mat are drawn to look like one.
